# 🏗️ LEARNING PLAN: DATA SCIENCE

### **MODULE 1: Computer Science Foundations** 🖥️
| Book                                            | Chapters | Objective                                                                                              |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Introduction to Computer Science (OpenStax)** | 1 to 8   | Master the transduction of reality into bits, hardware architecture, and operating system abstraction. |

### **MODULE 2: Logic & Programming Principles** 🧩
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Programming Logic and Design (Joyce Farrell)** | 1 to 10 | Learn to design processing flows and algorithms via pseudocode and flowcharts, independent of any programming language. |

### **MODULE 3: Python Implementation & Syntax** 🐍
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Introduction to Python Programming (OpenStax)** | 1 to 15 | Translate the logic from Module 2 into Python code: data structures, OOP, recursion, and file handling. |

### **MODULE 4: Extraction of Intelligence** 👁️🔨
| Book                                      | Chapters | Objective                                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------------------- | :------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Principles of Data Science (OpenStax)** | 1 to 10  | Master the data science cycle: from descriptive statistics and inference to reporting results and ethics.          |
| **Data Science for All (Brennan)**        | 1 to 10  | Understand the conceptual landscape of ML, including uncertainty, wrangling, and supervised/unsupervised learning. |

### **MODULE 5: Comprehensive Statistics** 📊
| Book                                                              | Chapters | Objective                                                                                                                   |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------- | :------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Statistics for Managers Using Microsoft Excel (Levine et al.)** | 00 to 20 | Master the full statistical spectrum: Descriptive, Probability, Distributions, and Inferential testing for decision-making. |

### **MODULE 6: Scientific Data Manipulation (Python)** 🧪
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Python Data Science Handbook (Jake VanderPlas)** | 1 to 50 | Master the Python ecosystem: NumPy (computation), Pandas (data), Matplotlib/Seaborn, and Scikit-Learn. |

### **MODULE 7: Statistical Exploration (R)** 📈
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R for Data Science (Hadley Wickham et al., 2nd Ed.)** | 1 to 29 | Master the Tidyverse for cleaning, transformation, and advanced graphical visualization of heterogeneous data. |

### **MODULE 8: Data Engineering (SQL)** 🗄️
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sams Teach Yourself SQL in 10 Minutes (Ben Forta)** | 1 to 22 | Master relational algebra: extraction, complex joins, subqueries, and transaction management. |

### **MODULE 9: Fundamental Linear Algebra** 📐
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linear Algebra and Its Applications (David C. Lay et al.)** | 1 to 6 | Understand mathematical foundations: linear systems, matrix algebra, determinants, and vector spaces. |

### **MODULE 10: Machine Learning Systems** 🤖
| Book                                                                                    | Chapters | Objective                                                                                  |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow (Aurélien Géron)** | Complete | Build end-to-end predictive models, from feature engineering to neural network deployment. |

### **MODULE 11: Optimization & Advanced Algebra** ⚡
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linear Algebra and Its Applications (David C. Lay et al.)** | 7 | Master Symmetric Matrices, Quadratic Forms, and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD). |
| **Calcul Différentiel (Fortin)** | 0 to 6 | Master the differential calculus foundations for gradient descent optimization. |
| **Calcul Intégral (Charron)** | 1 to 4 | Master integral calculus for continuous probability distributions and advanced ML theory. |

### **MODULE 12: Deep Learning Architectures** 🧠
| Book | Chapters | Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Math and Architectures of Deep Learning (Chaudhury)** | Complete | Understand the deep mathematical theory and structure of neural network architectures. |
| **Deep Learning with Python (François Chollet)** | Complete | Implement modern architectures (CNN, RNN, Transformers) using Keras and Python. |
